Refund Policy

This policy covers tickets sold on NOKTURA. For events imported from other websites, the selling website’s policy applies.

If the event is cancelled

When an organiser cancels an event in NOKTURA, every ticket is refunded automatically and in full — ticket price and service fee — to the buyer’s NOKTURA wallet within minutes. You can spend it or withdraw it to PayPal.

If the event is moved or changed significantly

If the date, venue or headline act changes materially, contact the organiser through the event chat or their profile. If you can’t resolve it within 7 days, write to support and we will review the case and may refund you from the organiser’s proceeds.

If you can’t go

Tickets are not refundable just because you can’t attend, unless the organiser says otherwise. You can send your ticket to a friend in the app, or list it on the market.

Market purchases

Payments between users on the market are final. If an item never arrives or is not as described, report the listing; we will review it and may suspend the seller.

Problems at the door

If a valid ticket was refused, ask staff to scan the code and note the time, then contact support within 48 hours.
