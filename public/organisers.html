Organiser Terms

These terms apply in addition to the Terms of Service when you post an event or sell tickets on NOKTURA.

Your responsibilities

  • You run the event: venue, permits, licences, security, health and safety, age checks and any taxes due on your ticket income.
  • Describe the event accurately — date, time, venue, line-up, age limit, price — and keep it up to date.
  • Honour every valid ticket. Use the Door view to scan QR codes; each one works once.

Money

  • Buyers pay the ticket price plus a service fee. You receive the full ticket price in your NKT wallet the moment each ticket sells, and can withdraw it to PayPal.
  • If you cancel, every ticket is refunded in full. The ticket price is taken back from your wallet, so keep enough balance until the event has happened; you can’t cancel with tickets sold if your balance doesn’t cover the refunds.
  • We may hold your proceeds if we receive credible reports that the event is fake, misdescribed or didn’t take place, until it is resolved.

Data

You can see the handle and ticket code of each ticket holder for check-in. Use it only to run the event; don’t export it for marketing without the holder’s consent.

Promotion

You can promote your event from its page. Promoted events are labelled and shown first in their city.
