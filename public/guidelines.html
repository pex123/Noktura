Community Guidelines

NOKTURA is for going out and meeting people. Keep it that way.

Be real

  • Use one account, and don’t pretend to be someone else, a venue or a brand.
  • Only post events that are actually happening and that you have the right to promote.
  • Don’t buy fake engagement or sell accounts.

Be safe

  • No threats, harassment, stalking or hate based on who someone is.
  • No sexual content involving anyone under 18 — reported to authorities.
  • Only share someone else’s location, photo or contact details with their consent.
  • No sale or promotion of drugs, weapons or other illegal goods and services.

Be fair with money and tickets

  • Don’t resell tickets you don’t have, or the same ticket twice.
  • No scams, phishing, pyramid schemes or requests for money under false pretences.
  • Never send NKT to someone you don’t know because they promised you something.

What happens when rules are broken

Anyone can report a post, comment, message, pin, listing, event or account. Our team reviews reports and may remove content, limit features, hold payments under investigation, or suspend accounts. Serious cases are passed to the police.

Look after each other

If you or a friend is in danger, call 112 (EU) or 192 (Serbia, police) / 194 (ambulance). Drink water, plan your way home, and look out for the people around you.
