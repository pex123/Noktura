Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data NOKTURA collects and why. The controller is [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME], [registered address], company no. [MB], tax no. [PIB]. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (ZZPL).

What we collect

  • Account: handle, password (stored only as a bcrypt hash), display name, photo, bio, city, age confirmation and the date you accepted the terms.
  • Activity: posts, comments, likes, events you create or RSVP to, tickets, pins, market listings, chats and files you share in them.
  • Payments: your NKT balance and every transaction. PayPal processes card and PayPal payments; we receive the order ID, amount and status, and for withdrawals the PayPal email you enter — never your card number.
  • Location: the city you choose; the exact spot of pins you drop; and your live position only while you share it.
  • Technical: IP address and request logs for security and rate limiting, kept for up to 30 days.

Why we use it

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • To keep accounts and payments safe and prevent fraud (legitimate interest; legal obligation).
  • To keep financial records (legal obligation).
  • To show live location to friends (your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by switching it off).

We don’t sell personal data and don’t use it for third-party advertising. Promoted content is placed by city and context, not by profiling.

Who sees what

Your profile and public posts are visible to everyone. Friends-only pins and posts are shown only to friends. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. Organisers see the handle and ticket code of their ticket holders. Service providers who process data for us: our hosting provider (application and database), PayPal (payments), OpenStreetMap / CARTO (map tiles) and, if enabled, Ticketmaster (event listings — no user data is sent).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account, except payment records, which we keep for the period required by accounting and anti-money-laundering law (up to 10 years), and security logs (30 days).

Your rights

You can access, correct, export, restrict or delete your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Most of this is in Settings (download my data, delete account). For anything else write to support@noktura.app. You can complain to the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection of Serbia (poverenik.rs) or your local EU authority.

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed. Payment operations run in database transactions with idempotency keys so nothing is charged twice. Database access is restricted to the application server.

Storage on your device

We store your session token, chosen city and theme in your browser’s local storage. We don’t use advertising cookies.
